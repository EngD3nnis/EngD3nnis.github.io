This is the beginning of something I've been meaning to do for a while: writing things down.

I'm Dennis — most people online know me as **DNX**. I'm a Data Science student at the University of Nairobi, and I spend most of my time working on AI engineering, machine learning, and building software that does something useful.

## Why a blog?

Writing forces clarity. When you try to explain gradient descent or backpropagation to someone else, you quickly discover the gaps in your own understanding. This blog is where I'll work through ideas, document what I'm learning, and share things I think are worth sharing.

## What to expect

- **AI and ML** — theory, implementation, practical engineering
- **Mathematics** — the parts that matter for building intelligent systems
- **Software engineering** — clean code, good systems, things that work
- **Learning in public** — mistakes included

No filler. No SEO bait. Just notes from someone who's building.

If you want to follow along, the [RSS feed](/blog/feed.xml) is the best way. You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/EngD3nnis) or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/_dxnnoh).
